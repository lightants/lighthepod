Process

Four steps. Then it’s on the calendar.

  1. You record

    Riverside, Zoom, or local files. Interview, solo, or two-speaker. We never need the booth — only the session.

  2. You send

    A link, a package, guest-name spellings, and intro/outro if you have them. First episode is invoiced before work starts.

    Cleanup, mix, master. Notes and clips when booked. The hours after US evenings are studio hours — that is the overnight.

  4. You publish

    WAV/MP3 for audio. SRT on clips. 1080p H.264 and a PNG thumbnail on Stage. One revision in scope.

FAQ

Straight answers before you send files.

How fast is turnaround?

Five business days from a complete handoff — files, names, and package. 48-hour rush is +40%. Relay works the hours after US evenings; that coverage is how rush can land the next morning.

What files do I send and get back?

Send a Riverside or Zoom link, or local WAV/MP3/video. Cut returns mixed WAV + MP3, loudness-matched for podcasts, with intro/outro if supplied. Show adds timestamped notes, a cleaned transcript, titles/description, and three vertical captioned clips (MP4 + SRT). Stage adds a 1080p H.264 talking-head master, five clips, and one PNG thumbnail.

What loudness spec do you master to?

Common podcast loudness: about −16 LUFS stereo / −19 LUFS mono, true peak under −1 dBTP. Video masters follow YouTube-safe peaks.

How many speakers and how long?

Packages assume two speakers and up to 60 minutes finished (about 90 minutes raw). Extra speaker or extra camera is +$79. Extra 30 minutes of raw is +$45.

How many revisions?

One revision is in scope. Mix notes and small picture cuts — not a recut of the conversation. Further rounds are quoted.

Who owns the episode?

You do. Relay keeps project files for 30 days, then discards them. See terms.
