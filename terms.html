Working terms

How an episode is handled.

Plain terms for a paid post-production job. Last updated 28 August 2026.

The work

Relay Studio provides remote podcast post-production: audio edit, mix, and master; and, when booked, show notes, transcript cleanup, short-form clips, talking-head video edit, and a thumbnail. You record. Relay does not operate a walk-in studio, book guests, or sell ads.

You own the episode

You retain all rights in the finished episode, notes, clips, and thumbnail delivered under the invoice. Relay claims no ownership in your show, guests, or masters. Relay may keep an internal copy only as needed to complete the job and the revision window.

Project files

Session files, working timelines, and raw copies Relay holds are kept for 30 days after delivery, then discarded. If you need a project file handoff, ask during that window. After 30 days, only what you already received is guaranteed to exist.

Scope and revision

Each package includes one revision. A revision means mix notes, small picture cuts, caption fixes, or copy tweaks on the materials already produced. It is not a recut of the conversation, a new guest, or a change of package after delivery. Extra rounds, extra length, extra speakers, extra cameras, extra clips, and 48-hour rush are quoted as listed on the pricing page — or, if they fall outside those add-ons, quoted before the work continues.

Turnaround

Standard turnaround is five business days from a complete handoff (files, names, package, and paid invoice). 48-hour rush is +40% of the episode package and is confirmed in writing before it starts. Relay works the hours after typical US evenings; that coverage is how rush can land the next morning. Holidays and incomplete files pause the clock.

Invoicing

Prices are in US dollars. The first episode is paid before work starts. Retainers are invoiced monthly. Add-ons appear on the same invoice or a follow-up invoice before delivery. Refunds are not issued for work already delivered in scope. If Relay cannot start a paid job, the unused fee is returned.

Your materials

You confirm you have the right to send the session, music beds, and guest appearance. You are responsible for releases and platform rules. Relay will not publish on your channels unless you book YouTube upload as an add-on, and then only as a paste of the files and description you approved.

Quiet on the work

Relay does not use your episode as a public case study unless you ask. A sample reel is available on request from other material you approve, or from studio-made examples.
